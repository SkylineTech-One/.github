**Dandara AI** is an intelligent agent suite designed to transform the way small and medium businesses sell, serve, and grow.

More than a chatbot — it is an **autonomous sales agent** working 24/7 for your business.

```
Customer sends a WhatsApp message
        ↓
Dandara AI analyses intent
        ↓
Responds, qualifies, negotiates and closes
        ↓
Payment processed via Multicaixa Express
        ↓
You wake up with sales already done.
```
